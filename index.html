Lumen
Privacy policy

Your money.
Your phone.
Nothing else.

Lumen is an iPhone app that tracks your spending from the payment alerts your bank already sends you. Everything stays on your phone.

In private testing · not yet on the App Store

What makes it different

👤

No account

There is no sign-up and no Lumen login. Install it and start.

📱

No server

Your payments are read and stored on your iPhone. The developer never receives them.

⚡

No typing

Payments appear on their own, filed by category, with paid and received clearly marked.

How it works

  1. 1
    Your bank alerts youBy text message or email, as it already does for every payment.
  2. 2
    Lumen reads the alert on your phoneTexts are passed to Lumen by a Shortcuts automation that you switch on. Emails are optional: if you connect Gmail, your phone fetches alerts from bank and card senders directly from Google.
  3. 3
    The payment is saved on your phoneLumen keeps the amount, who it was to or from, the date and the category. Messages that are not payments are discarded.

If you connect Gmail

It is optional. Lumen works with bank texts alone.

Lumen asks Google for read-only access to your mail. Google does not offer a narrower permission, so its screen says Lumen can read your email. Lumen uses that access only to find payment alerts from banks and card issuers, and it processes them on your iPhone.

Email content is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. You can remove Lumen's access at any time in the app or in your Google account. The full details are in the privacy policy.
