Questions

Fair questions.

Can you see my payments?

No. There's no server for them to land on. They never leave your iPhone.

How does it read my bank texts?

Apple doesn't let apps read your messages, which is a good thing. You switch on one automation in Apple's Shortcuts app. It passes your texts to Lumen on your phone, and Lumen keeps only the payment alerts.

Do I have to connect my Gmail?

No, that part is optional. If you do, your iPhone fetches bank alerts straight from Google and reads them on the device. Google's screen will say "read your email" because it has no smaller option. The privacy policy has the details.
